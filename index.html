A note before you begin

The Abbotts of Broadford & the Hunter

Five centuries condensed onto one canvas

This tree traces your family from an Irish constable's house in Broadford, County Clare, through the Hunter Valley pastoral dynasty founded by John Kingsmill Abbott, the colonial political dynasty led by your great-great-grandfather Sir Joseph Palmer Abbott, KCMG, and on through the Senator, the soldier, the novelist, the Northern Territory Administrator, and the grazier of Tooloogan Vale — your grandfather William Edward Macartney Abbott — down to you.

Click any name to read their full biography. The gold-outlined boxes are your direct line of descent. Use the search box to jump to anyone, and the zoom controls to navigate.
